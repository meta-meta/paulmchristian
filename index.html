Research hub

Musicality.computer

A place for refactoring music.

Musicality.computer is the home for my experiments with exotic approaches to working with music theory, alternate notation systems, instrument design, and embodied ways of thinking through sound.

It acts as a host for prototypes, sketches, and playable research: tools that treat theory as something you can touch, move through, retune, and reorganize.
